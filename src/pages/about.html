Who are we?

We are an interdisciplinar team with professionals to cover all aspects of the platform. We count in our team experienced developers and clinical psychologists.
If you are interested in our research, contact us!

Geotec is a research group specialized in geospatial technologies and GIS development (Geographic Information Systems). We address everyday problems from a geospatial perspective, tackling these complex challenges through a multidisciplinary, innovative, and collaborative approach, exploring different theoretical and practical perspectives to provide effective and sustainable solutions to social and environmental issues such as mobility, transportation, population aging, environmental health, climate change, and education.

LabPsiTec (Laboratory of Psychology and Technology) at the Jaume I University of Castellón and the University of Valencia has been researching for over 25 years the possibilities that New Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) offer to Clinical Psychology. These tools (Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality, Internet, mobile systems, etc.) can be used for assessment, treatment, and the study of basic psychological processes. Additionally, through the Psychological Assistance Service at Jaume I University, our patients benefit from the latest advances in psychological treatments.

Principal investigators

Sven Casteleyn

Sven Casteleyn is an associate professor at the University Jaime I (UJI), Castellón, Spain. He previously held a five year Ramón y Cajal fellowship (UJI) and a two year European Marie Curie individual fellowship (Universidad Politécnica de Valencia), and various post- and pre-doctoral research positions at the Vrije Universiteit Brussel, from which he holds a Ph.D. and a Master degree. His research interests lie primarily within the broad field of Web engineering, mobile computing, geographic information science and technology applied to various applications fields, primarily mental health.

Carlos Granell Canut

Carlos Granell Canut is associate professor at UJI. He previously held a 5-year Ramón y Cajal post-doctoral fellowship, worked for 3 years as a postdoc in the Digital Earth and Reference Data Unit of the European Commission’s Joint Research Centre (Italy), and was a post- and pre-doctoral researcher during the period 2003-2010 at the Universitat Jaume I of Castellón, from which he holds a Ph.D. (2006). His research interests lie in multi-disciplinary GIS, spatial analysis & visualization applied to mental health and mobility, and reproducibility research practice in GIScience.

Juana María Bretón López

Juana María Bretón López has a PhD in Psychology from the University of Granada (year 2008) and is currently an associate professor at the Universitat Jaume I since 2018. The central theme of her research is the use of Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) for the application of psychological treatments and the promotion of health and well-being. She belongs to the Labpsitec research group directed by Dr. Azucena García Palacios.

Patricia Gual Montolio

Patricia Gual Montolio holds a PhD in Psychology from Universitat Jaume I (UJI). She is faculty/research staff and, since 2018, a member of LABPSITEC led by Prof. Azucena García Palacios. Her work bridges psychology and technology to enhance psychotherapy, using a LABPSITEC-validated mobile app (EmoGuía/Mi Emi/Emotional Well-being) to assess patients daily, trigger real-time clinical alerts, and deliver personalized EMIs informed by EMA to guide treatment. She teaches Personality, Assessment and Psychological Treatment, and since 2019 collaborates with UJI’s Psychological Assistance Service on evaluation, diagnosis, and individual and group interventions.
